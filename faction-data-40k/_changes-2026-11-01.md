## 40k (faction-data-40k)
- Points re-synced to MFM v1.6: 377 values changed across 29 files. Examples: Intercessor Squad 80 -> 85, Boyz 90 -> 85, Beast Snagga Boyz 85 -> 80, Squighog Boyz 140 -> 130, Breaka Boyz 135 -> 120, Custodian Guard Sodality 240 for 3.
- `pointsTiers` added to 440 units that MFM prices per copy (`points` is still the first-copy price).
- `models` set to the smallest MFM unit size: 370 units, 346 of them were `models: 1`.
- Renamed to their MFM names (24): Custodian Guard -> Custodian Guard Sodality, Prosecutors/Vigilators/Witchseekers -> ... Squad, Sagittarum Custodians -> Sagittarum Custodians Sodality, Rukkatrukk Squigbuggy -> Rukkatrukk Squigbuggies, Wartrakk -> Wartrakks, Invader ATV -> Invader ATVs, Ancient in Terminator Armor -> Armour, Eradicator Squad -> Eradicator Squad with Melta Rifles, Vypers -> Vyper (Craftworlds, Drukhari), Sentry Pylon -> Sentry Pylons, Hellblade -> Hell Blade (DG/TS/WE), titans in Chaos Knights and Chaos Daemons -> Chaos <Titan>.
- Empty M/T/Sv/W/Ld/OC lines filled from BSData: 103 units (e.g. Battle Sisters Squad).
- Truncated abilities completed from BSData: 885 strings. 803 are plain completions of the cut-off text. 83 carry the current 11e wording because the BSData text differs after the first words.
- Removed the duplicate Wolf Guard Headtakers entry that held the Hunting Wolves profile (M10", Sv6+, W1).
- New `Dark Angels.rules.json`: the 3 DA-only detachments (Darkflight Pursuit, Inner Circle Task Force, Wrath of the Rock) with DP and enhancement costs from MFM, effects paraphrased from BSData, plus Combat Doctrines.
- `Ultramarines.rules.json`: armyRules now has Combat Doctrines (paraphrased, BSData rule 3b76-4053-ece9-6e7d).

## Age of Sigmar (faction-data)
- 11 within-faction duplicates resolved. 10 were BSData "Undersize Unit" (1-2 model) variants: I kept the full-size reinforceable entry. The 11th was an identical second copy of Kragnos. Points corrected to BSData: Crypt Horrors 160 -> 150, Crypt Flayers 160 -> 140, Stormdrake Guard 310 -> 290, Kragnos (Sons of Behemat) 590 -> 580.
- Removed 26 non-unit 0-point entries: 23 "Battle Traits" pseudo-units (their traits are already in each .rules.json `battleTraits`), Delusion Tracker, and 2 empty Orruk Anvil of Apotheosis stubs.
- Kept 46 real 0-point entries: faction terrain, companion units (e.g. Toll's Companions, Neave's Companions) and Anvil of Apotheosis heroes (BSData has no base cost; their points come from the options chosen).

## MESBG (faction-data-mesbg)
- Removed 3 identical duplicate copies: The Abyssal Knight, The Slayer of Men (Rise of the Necromancer), Khandish King (Variags of Khand).

## Not changed (no unambiguous source)
- 40k units missing from MFM v1.6: 61 [Crucible] units, 137 Legends/fortification entries with no MFM listing, and 34 others. These include the Custodes units that MFM now splits into two weapon variants, Orks Speed Freeks vehicles, Tactical/Devastator/Suppressor Squad, Pedro Kantor and Uriel Ventris. Captain Sicarius and Lieutenant Titus keep their old names because Cato Sicarius and Captain Titus already exist in the file.
- 7 units still have empty stats: Aquila/Decimus Kill Team (mixed profiles), 4 Searchlights, Deffkoptas with Big Shootas [Legends].
- About 300 ability strings are still truncated. BSData has no matching ability of the same name for them, or the wording has changed.
- MESBG: Ranger of the North has two entries in Battle of Fornost (Minor Hero vs Independent Hero) and no source settles which is right. Tom Bombadil and Goldberry W "?" is the official value (BSData agrees). Bard's Family W "n/a" is a composite of three models, and the stats are in its rule text. Moria Goblin Drummer at 0 pts is correct because it comes with the Moria Goblin Drum.
